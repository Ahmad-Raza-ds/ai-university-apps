Standard 4.0 Grading Scale & Formula

This calculator uses the standard 4.00 university grading scale adopted internationally.

Letter Grade Grade Point Value Description
A4.00Outstanding / Excellent
A-3.67Very Good
B+3.33Good / High Honors
B3.00Above Average
B-2.67Average
C+2.33Satisfactory
C2.00Passing (Core Minimum)

D1.00Bare Minimum Pass
F0.00Fail (0.00 Points)

Official Formulas Used:

Quality Points: Quality Points = Credit Hours × Grade Point
Semester GPA: Semester GPA = Total Semester Quality Points ÷ Total Semester Credits
Overall CGPA: CGPA = (Prior Quality Points + Semester Quality Points) ÷ Total Cumulative Credits
